MHGU Bingo

Pick a grid size, choose which pools fill the squares, then hit New Card. Click a square when you've done it. Complete a row, column or diagonal for a bingo.

Pools

  • Monsters — hunt a monster at a given rank, e.g. Hunt Rathalos (High Rank). A monster huntable at more than one rank gets a square for each.
  • Weapons — clear anything with a given weapon and style.
  • Objectives — capture something, clear a Hyper quest, no-faint runs.
  • Custom — whatever you add yourself.

Goals are always checked against your filters first, so a card will never ask for something you've excluded, or for a rank you've filtered out.

Seeds

Every card has a seed like MHGU-5F-M4W3O2C3-K7T2NX-9C4A, shown in the title bar. Paste it back to rebuild the same card. It carries your grid size and pool weights, but not your filters or custom entries — so if a friend's settings differ, their card will differ too, and you'll see a warning saying so.

To share the exact card, including custom squares, open the Twitch button and hit Publish this card. That gives you a short link which always rebuilds it perfectly, however different the other person's settings are.

Twitch

The Twitch button holds two ready-made bot commands. There's no account and nothing to log into — they're just URLs for a chat bot to fetch.

  • !bingo rolls a brand-new card for whoever runs it and prints the seed alongside the link. It works on its own, with nothing published first.
  • !currentcard points at the stream's current card, for when chat should follow along with yours.
  • !setcurrentcard rolls a new card and makes it the stream's. Restrict it to moderators.

Log in with Twitch and your channel is filled in for you, so the commands come out complete — copy, paste, done, with nothing in them to edit. They keep working however many cards you make.

Cards rolled by the bot use the default pools and filters, which is why the seed it prints reproduces exactly for anyone opening this app fresh.

Rerolling

Hover a square and hit ↻ to swap just that goal. Once you do, the seed no longer rebuilds the card — it's marked edited and you'll need a share link to pass it on.

Marks and settings are saved in this browser. Printing (Ctrl‑P) gives a clean black-and-white card.

  • Collection Tracker Tracks which weapons, armor and Palico gear you own, and what it costs to finish them.

!bingo — a fresh card for whoever asks

Rolls a brand-new card and replies with a link plus its seed, so chat can either open that exact board or rebuild it here. Works on its own — nothing to publish first.

!currentcard — the card the stream is playing

Points chat at your stream's current card so they can follow along.

!setcurrentcard — roll a new one for the stream

Rolls a fresh board and makes it the stream's card, so !currentcard immediately points at the new one. Restrict this to moderators — there's no secret in the URL, so the bot's own permissions are what stop a viewer rerolling your card.

Put the card on screen up for chat

Sends the board currently on screen to your channel, for when you've built one with your own custom pool rather than rolling from chat.
