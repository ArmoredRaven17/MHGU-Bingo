MHGU Quest Bingo

Pick a grid size, choose which pools fill the squares, then hit New Card. Click a square when you've done it. Complete a row, column or diagonal for a bingo.

Pools

  • Monsters — hunt a monster at a given rank, e.g. Hunt Rathalos (High Rank). A monster huntable at more than one rank gets a square for each.
  • Weapons — clear anything with a given weapon and style, plus one square per Prowler bias when Prowler is included.
  • Objectives — one per box in the Objectives panel: capture something, clear a Hyper quest, clear a Special Permit. Untick a box and its square goes too.
  • Talismans — something worth melding for: a rarity by name, a slot count, or a skill. Never skill points or two-skill combos — those are a grind rather than a square you tick off. The skills offered are the nine that can roll on any talisman.
  • Custom — whatever you add yourself.

Goals are always checked against your filters first, so a card will never ask for something you've excluded, or for a rank you've filtered out.

Colours

A square's outline tells you which filter put it there.

Weapon squares use that weapon's own colour instead:

Marking a square keeps its colour but lightens it and draws a cross through it. A completed line overrides all of that and fills the squares solid, in a colour picked to contrast with whatever theme you're using.

Seeds

Every card has a seed like MHGU-5F-M4W3O2C3-K7T2NX-9C4A, shown in the title bar. Paste one back to rebuild that card.

The seed carries your grid size and pool weights, but not your filters or your custom entries — there's no room. So what happens when someone else loads it depends on how the card was made:

  • Made on the default pools and filters — which is every card the bot rolls — it rebuilds exactly for anyone, even someone who has since customised their own settings. The app spots that and rebuilds using the defaults.
  • Made with your own filters or custom squares, it can't be rebuilt from 28 characters. It still generates, and a banner says plainly that it may not match.

To share a card whose squares are exactly yours, set your channel under the Twitch button and use Use the card you're looking at. That sends the board on screen to your channel, so !currentcard and its link show it — custom squares and all, whatever settings the person opening it has.

Twitch

Three ready-made bot commands, under the Twitch button. Log in and your channel is filled in for you, so they come out complete — copy, paste, done, with nothing in them to edit. They keep working however many cards you make.

  • !bingo rolls a brand-new card for whoever runs it and prints the seed alongside the link. It needs nothing set up first.
  • !currentcard points at the stream's current card, for when chat should follow along with yours.
  • !setcurrentcard rolls a new card and makes it the stream's. Restrict this one to moderators — anyone who can run it can reroll your card.

Cards the bot rolls use the default pools and filters, which is why the seed it prints reproduces exactly for whoever pastes it.

Rerolling

Hover a square and hit ↻ to swap just that goal. Once you do, the seed no longer rebuilds the card — it's marked edited and you'll need a share link to pass it on.

Marks and settings are saved in this browser. Printing (Ctrl‑P) gives a clean black-and-white card.

Other MHGU Apps

Companion fan tools by the same author. Each opens in a new tab.

  • Quest Randomizer Rolls random quests when you can't decide what to hunt.
  • Collection Tracker Tracks which weapons, armor and Palico gear you own, and what it costs to finish them.
  • Equipment Box Mirrors the in-game box — 2000 hunter slots and 1000 Palico — so you can plan where things sit.
  • Bingo you are here Builds a bingo card of hunting goals, with a shareable seed so a group can race the same board.
  • Hunting Log Keeps a running record of your hunts — what you were after, what you wore, who came along.
  • Zenny Gauntlet A scored challenge run — fail a hunt or get carted and you lose that weapon/style combo for the rest of the run.
  • Set Builder Assemble a full set by hand and see exactly which skills it activates.
  • Weapon Trees Browse every weapon upgrade tree, with full stats on any node you click.
  • Challenge Run A permadeath challenge run — start from a root weapon, lose it on any quest failure, clear every Key Quest.
  • Charm Farm A Clicker/Idle game about beating up various Brachydios until you finally get the desired God Charms you always wanted.
  • Talisman Bingo A bingo card of talisman conditions — keep drawing charms until one finally fills the line you needed.
  • Fishing A fishing sim on the game's own tables — prepare at camp, travel to a locale, come home with the catch.
  • Armor Viewer Renders a hunter in 3D with every armor piece swappable, so you can mix and match a set.

Logged in as . The commands below are filled in and ready to paste as-is.

!bingo — a fresh card for whoever asks

Rolls a brand-new card and replies with a link plus its seed, so chat can either open that exact board or rebuild it here. Works on its own — nothing to publish first.

!currentcard — the card the stream is playing

Points chat at your stream's current card so they can follow along.

!setcurrentcard — roll a new one for the stream

Rolls a fresh board and makes it the stream's card, so !currentcard immediately points at the new one. Restrict this to moderators — there's no secret in the URL, so the bot's own permissions are what stop a viewer rerolling your card.

Use the card you're looking at

Makes the board currently on screen your stream's card, so !currentcard points at it. Use this when you've built one with your own filters or custom squares — !setcurrentcard rolls from the defaults and can't reproduce those. It's a snapshot, so send again after making changes.
